Why is the race to backwardness India's biggest nightmare?

When I see the race to be more Backward in India, I think of what Swami Vivekananda ji said:

"The weak have no place here, in this life or any other life. Weakness leads to slavery. Weakness leads to all kinds of misery, physical and mental. Weakness is death."

There is nothing that is worse than "weakness"; however, today, being weaker in India is a superpower.

The weaker you show yourself, the more the government will try to empower you.

The stronger you are, the more your government will try to undermine you, disrespect you, and you will be hated by weaker communities.

Look at the Brahmins

They are considered to be thought leaders, fairly well economically, and are at the top of the varnas.

Yet we see slogans like "Brahminwad Murdabad"; Brahmin women are openly abused by many of these so-called Dalit and OBC leaders, and the Modi government doesn't say a word.

These were the same OBC and Dalit leaders who were abusing the Muslims 5-7 years ago.

Booking Ajeet Bharti under the Modi Act is just one example.

Who is not against the Brahmins today?

Leftists, Bhim Army, CJP guys, to some extent AAP, Congress, SP, LJP, RJD; is there anyone left?

Is there any political party left for the Brahmins other than the BJP, where they can be sidelined and slapped biweekly?

Shekhar Gupta has correctly said:

Brahmins' vote is the same for the BJP, as the Muslims' vote is for the Congress.

So, the lines of Swami Vivekananda ji, prove to be wrong here.

Because in India, the weaker you are economically and socially, the stronger you become politically.

Under the Modi-Shah BJP, the weak have a place in India; it's in the 50% of reserved seats in Government jobs and Educational institutions, and it comes with the right to abuse the Upper Caste Hindu's and the Muslims pari passu.
